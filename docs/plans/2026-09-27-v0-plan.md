# abusekit v0 plan — ship, integrate with e2a, evaluate on the incident

Goal: a running abusekit scoring e2a accounts in shadow within two weeks, evaluated against the
September 2026 phishing campaigns. v0 is the subset of the r2 design that produces a trustworthy
score for e2a; everything else is deferred, not dropped.

## v0 scope

In: event ingest with static redaction and signed auth; same-tenant links; Postgres store;
features (velocity, lifetime totals, permanent onboarding facts, name patterns, fan-out,
self-send-before-external, burst ratio, linked features); the deterministic **local** scorer;
`Plan`/`Combine`; worker with dirty_seq, rescore-at, priority, budgets; `GET /v1/subjects/{id}`,
`POST /v1/subjects/{id}/evaluate`, `POST /v1/events`, `POST /v1/labels`; the **gemini** adapter
(shadow only) and the **jev** adapter (features-only, shadow only, behind the allowlist); the
harness (`abusekit eval`, `abusekit score --jsonl`, cassettes, manifests, floors) and the CI gate;
e2a emitter (OSS, config-gated, River outbox) and billing sidecar payment events; a private
operator backfill script in the ops repo.

Out (v1): laya adapter, webhooks, LLM explainer, promotion tooling, calibration maps for vendor
scorers (v0 vendor rules are shadow, so uncalibrated is acceptable and the harness reports it). The
list and erasure endpoints were originally scoped here too, but are now S3b below (still v0, own
design pass) rather than deferred to v1 outright.

## Evaluation on the incident (S9)

Positives: the operator accounts from the September campaigns (paused accounts in e2a, plus the
purged incarnations reconstructed from the durable ledgers and Cloud Logging). Negatives: a random
sample of ≥300 accounts created in the same 60 days with no pause, no complaint and ≥1 send.
For each account, events are reconstructed from e2a's tables into abusekit's vocabulary with real
timestamps; the corpus lives in private storage only (public-GitHub data boundary).
Metrics: precision/recall at the local rule's threshold, calibration, and **lead time** = seconds
between the `high` verdict and the account's first external send (negative means too late).
Success for v0: every positive at `high` before its first external send with the local scorer,
≤2% of negatives at `high`, and the churn incarnations from the third onward at `high` on first
`resource.created`.
